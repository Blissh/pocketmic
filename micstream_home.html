Windows Integration

What Micstream does

Micstream installs a tiny driver that makes your phone appear like a standalone microphone on Windows — recognized by Zoom, Discord, OBS, or any other Windows application.

It also removes background noise: DeepFilterNet, an AI noise-cancellation model, runs on your PC and filters out keyboard clicks, fans, and room noise. It's on by default and free.
