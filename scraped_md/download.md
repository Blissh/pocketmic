Download Micstream
==================

You need 2 things:

* A client for your PC
* The app for your phone

PC client
---------

### Micstream for Windows

The installer sets up the client and the Micstream audio driver. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

[Download for Windows](/downloads/MicstreamSetup.exe) [Setup Guide](./guides/use-phone-as-mic-windows)

### Micstream for Linux

One small service that registers a virtual microphone with PipeWire. No kernel driver needed.

[Download .deb](/downloads/micstream-amd64.deb)

Debian, Ubuntu, Mint

[Download .rpm](/downloads/micstream-x86_64.rpm)

Fedora

[Download tarball](/downloads/micstream-linux-x64.tar.gz)

Arch and any other distro (no root)

[Setup guide →](./guides/use-phone-as-mic-linux) [SHA-256 checksums](/downloads/micstream-linux-sha256sums.txt)

A macOS client is in development.
