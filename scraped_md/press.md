About Micstream
---------------

Micstream turns the phone you already own into a microphone for your PC. The phone app
streams your voice over Wi-Fi or a USB cable to the Micstream desktop client, where a custom
Windows audio driver — or a PipeWire service on Linux — presents it as a regular microphone.
That means it works in every app that accepts a mic input: Zoom, Teams, Google Meet,
Discord, OBS, games, dictation and recording software.

Audio is captured at 48 kHz in 32-bit, encoded with the low-latency Opus codec and protected
against packet loss with forward error correction. Built-in noise cancellation based on
DeepFilterNet removes keyboard clatter, fans and street noise in real time — it is on by
default and free. Micstream itself is free to use: a short ad unlocks an hour of streaming,
and Micstream Pro removes ads and time limits as a monthly, yearly or one-time purchase.

Micstream is developed by Andreas Toth and published by StreamDev Technologies OÜ in
Tallinn, Estonia. The Android app is on Google Play, the iPhone app on the App Store since
September 2026, and the Windows and Linux clients are free downloads at micstream.io.

### In one sentence

Micstream turns an Android phone or iPhone into a PC microphone for Windows and Linux — over
Wi-Fi or USB, with built-in AI noise cancellation.

Product
:   Micstream

Tagline
:   Your phone as a PC microphone

Phone app
:   Android 8.0 or later (Google Play) · iOS 18 or later (App Store)

Desktop client
:   Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, with a custom audio driver · Linux through PipeWire (.deb, .rpm, tarball)

Connection
:   Wi-Fi or USB cable; the PC is discovered automatically

Audio
:   48 kHz / 32-bit, Opus codec, forward error correction, 5–10 ms algorithmic delay

Noise cancellation
:   DeepFilterNet-based, on by default, free

Pricing
:   Free with ads — one short ad unlocks an hour of streaming. Micstream Pro removes ads and time limits: monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase, from $1.99 a month
